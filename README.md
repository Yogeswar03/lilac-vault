# LilacLove 💞 Our Private Love Sanctuary 🌸💜
> An intimate, romantic memory capsule & private chat strictly designed for **two lovers in love**.

Built with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Framer Motion**, **Tailwind CSS**, and an in-browser **HTML5 Canvas Video Synthesizer**.

---

## 💖 Key Features

### 1. 🔒 Strict 2-User Pairing Protocol
* Host creates the sanctuary and receives a 6-character secret code (e.g. `LOVE-777`).
* Partner enters code to bind. Once both are paired, the vault permanently **locks** to all third parties (`Strictly 2 Lovers Locked 💍`).

### 2. 💌 Private Love Chat & Whispers
* Real-time sweet chat channel just for the two of you.
* Tap **"Send Heart Burst ❤️"** to shower both screens in floating heart confetti.
* One-tap sweet whispers (*"Thinking of you 💖"*, *"Sending 1,000 kisses! 💋"*).

### 3. 🎬 AI Love Reel Maker
* Automatically compiles your favorite couple photos into an animated vertical 9:16 story.
* Dynamic **Ken Burns pan & zoom**, romantic pastel sparkles, and custom title cards.
* Synthesized **Lofi Chime Soundtrack** (Cmaj9 → Am9 → Fmaj7) via Web Audio API.
* 1-Click download as a `.webm` video!

### 4. 📱 Mobile PWA Installation
* Works as a standalone native-feeling app on iPhone and Android (no app store download required).

## 📱 How to Install on Mobile (iPhone & Android)

### On iPhone (Safari):
1. Open the website link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add**. The cute `💞 LilacLove` icon will appear directly on your home screen and open in full-screen mode like a native app!

### On Android (Chrome):
1. Open the website link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. Confirm. It now launches without browser address bars!

## 💾 Storage Capacity & Numbers

### 1. Local IndexedDB Storage (Out of the Box)
* Modern browsers grant **up to 60% of your device's free disk space** to IndexedDB.
* On typical phones with 64GB–256GB storage, the browser allocates **10GB to 50GB+** for app data.
* **Our Client-Side WebP Compression**: Each high-res photo is automatically optimized down to **~300KB** without visible quality loss.
* **Math**:
  * **1 GB of storage** ≈ **3,300+ full-resolution photos**!
  * **5 GB of storage** ≈ **16,000+ photos**!

### 2. Cloud Storage for 2 Remote Phones (Supabase Free Tier)
* To connect two separate physical phones across the internet:
  * **Supabase Free Tier**: Provides **1GB of free cloud media storage** + **500MB PostgreSQL database** (stores over 3,000 compressed photos completely free forever).
  * **Firebase Free Tier**: Provides **5GB of free storage** (~16,000 photos).

## 🚀 How to Deploy in 2 Minutes (Free)

1. Push this project to your GitHub repository.
2. Go to **[Vercel](https://vercel.com)** or **[Netlify](https://netlify.com)**.
3. Click **"Add New Project"** and select your GitHub repository.
4. Keep the default settings (Build Command: `npm run build`, Output Directory: `dist`).
5. Click **Deploy**! You will get an instant live HTTPS link (e.g. `https://our-love-story.vercel.app`) to share with your partner.
